## What's the point of a literature review?

- A literature review is the foundation of our knowledge system. It is the part of the research process where you deliberately and systematically explore existing research findings on a topic.
- Ideally, your new research will build on the findings of previous scholars. The earlier on in your research process you can do the literature review the better!

## What's metasynthesis?

- In simple terms, metasynthesis is like a more rigorous literature review that allows us to compare and contrast the findings from invidual research and studies into knowledge and insight. Metasynthesis typically involves the following key steps:
	- Identifying a reproducible search strategy, which itself involves steps like defining a target population, interevention of interest, relevant time frame
	- Creating a rubric to evaluate articles
	- Evaluating findings from search results and logging results in the evaluation matrix
	- Analysis and interpretation of the matrix findings
	- How does the current or proposed research support or build upon the findings from the research matrix?

> Metasynthesis can generate new findings from existing research all by itself, or it can be combined with fresh research to update the knowledge field on a topic.

## Key aspects of SHAMP literature review and metasynthesis

- **Reproducible process.** It is important to show your work, which means tracking:
  - Search strings/queries, including the phrases you cycled through
  - Where the search was conducted
  - When you searched
  - Number of results returned
  	> - Example:<br>
  			- "11/20/2026 PubMed <br>
  			- (community heart health education) AND (rural) Filters: in the last 5 years <br>
  			- Returns 287 results"

- **Defined keywords and search parameters** to support a multi-researcher search process.
	- Be flexible in your initial searching to get the right terms dialed in
	- Datasets should be appropriately sized to the number of researchers and time of the project, as balanced with accurantely covering the literature
