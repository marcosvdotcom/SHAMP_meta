## Where to search?

- **PubMed**
  - Turn on save search history to make your life easier!
- **Web of Science**
  - Turn on save search history!
- **Google Scholar**
  - Make sure your library account is connected!

## Tracking citations

Use **Zotero**!

## Strategies for keeping track of search processes

- Turn on save search history wherever possible.
- Create a tracking sheet for searches, recording:
  - Date searched
  - Where searched
  - Keywords/search string
  - Number of results returned
