## Metasynthesis

Metasynthesis is a method of research that allows us to generate new findings from existing studies. Key elements are:

- Identifying the key elements of concern, which might be:
  - A particular intervention
  - A study style or format
  - Target populations or key demographics
- Creating a comparison matrix to understand how individual studies relate to one another
- Synthesizing results from the comparison matrix to generate new understanding
  - You may then test how your own work or study aligns with those findings

## Key steps

1. **Identify a keyword set** that accurately represents the study set of concern.
2. **Create your comparison matrix.** Identify the key elements of the study of interest, then log data from the studies reviewed.

## Metasynthesis challenges

- Identifying and committing to your search string, then not deviating from it
- Search sets can vary in size and complexity. Even a strong search can still return hundreds of articles to review.
- Applying your evaluation rubric consistently, which is multiplied by having multiple researchers on the project
